I'm an FPGA engineer working on space payload hardware — sensor interfacing, high-speed data pipelines, and the embedded software that turns raw sensor output into usable data.

**FPGA Engineer, Digantara** (August 2024–present). Day to day this is RTL design and verification (VHDL/Verilog/SystemVerilog), high-speed interfaces (LVDS, MIPI-CSI, SERDES, DMA/AXI), clock-domain crossing, and bare-metal C on the processing side once data lands in memory.

Before Digantara I spent about a decade in hardware — astronomical instrumentation, ophthalmic medical devices, and industrial electronics. Full history on the [Career](/pages/career/) page.

M.Tech in Embedded Systems, BITS Pilani (2021–2023). B.Tech in Electronics & Communication, Sarada Institute of Science Technology and Management (2010–2014).

I write here about FPGA/HDL debugging patterns — the kind of thing that isn't in a textbook and mostly gets learned by getting burned once. General, tool-and-technique level, not tied to any specific employer's project.
